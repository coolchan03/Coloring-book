Android build of the coloring book, built from the same `web/` folder that powers the website.
Download **ColoringBook.apk** on your phone and open it (you may need to allow installs from your browser).
It installs over the previous version and keeps your pictures and coloring.

**What's new**
- Brushes with real texture: pencil, crayon, marker, paint, spray and sparkle. Pencil and crayon also shade the bucket fill like hand coloring.
- A new home screen with search, "Surprise me", recent pictures and seasonal picks.
- 117 pictures, including cute animals, ocean, space, food, vehicles and birthday.
- Flip to the previous or next picture with the arrows at the top, and your recently used colors are remembered.
- "Saved" confirmation, and the app now saves your last strokes when you switch away.
- More reliable drawing (a second finger or a resting palm no longer breaks a stroke) and a smaller download.
